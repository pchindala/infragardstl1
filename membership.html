Membership in InfraGard St. Louis

The InfraGard vetted membership is composed of trusted individuals from all 16 critical infrastructure sectors. Our members include business professionals, academics, state and local government representatives, law enforcement, and other key stakeholders committed to protecting the security of our region and the nation.

By becoming a member of the InfraGard St. Louis Alliance, you will gain access to exclusive security insights, training opportunities, and a trusted network that fosters public private collaboration to address today’s most pressing threats.

Benefits of Membership Include:

  • Collaboration across public and private sectors
  • Access to real time FBI threat alerts and intelligence briefings
  • Expert led webinars and security awareness trainings
  • Inclusion in secure information sharing platforms
  • Access to the Guardian FBI secure communication system
  • Educational resources and best practice guides
  • Opportunities to participate in Special Interest Groups (SIGs)
  • Free subscription to the InfraGard Magazine
  • Eligibility for GETS/WPS emergency communications programs
  • And best of all, membership is free
